Method · discovery engine & dependency atlasORVANTA GENOME2 / 15

276 feature pairs scanned: every strong dependency is explained by a rule or by rules combined

Research question: which fields depend on which — and is every strong dependency accounted for by a reconstructed rule?

SCAN 24 discretised features → 276 pairs; Cramér's V on a 1,000,406-row development half, re-scored on an independent half. 55 strong pairs: 42 explained by a rule · 13 mediated · 0 open.

Why significance is not enough
  • With 10M rows a 0.01-point difference has p ≈ 0; rules are accepted on Cramér's V, Cohen's w, exact-match share or KS distance against thresholds fixed in advance.
  • A pair counts as strong at V ≥ 0.05 on the development half and the validation half (999,594 rows).
  • Mediated = conditional V < 0.03 once country or route length is held fixed.

Conclusion

The generator is a compact set of mechanisms: route length ⌊Exp(1)⌋; conversions at every home-currency border; currencies drawn from the route; bank-level habits for cover, customer mix and speed; a 6 + 2 + 2 lognormal amount model; a local office clock with global weekly levels; and anomalies injected with clear signatures.

These are reconstructions consistent with the data — validated on independent splits — not the generator's source code. The data is synthetic and describes this generator, not real Swift traffic.
